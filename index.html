Parametric body

The body is a Revit family. The type holds measured sizes; the instance is one person. Formulas turn mobility and pose into the three numbers the engine walks with.

Eye height = if(seated, seat + 0.45·S, 0.936·S)

Clear width = shoulders + 0.25 ft; cane +0.5 ft; walker 2.5 ft; wheelchair 32 in

Parametric brain

VA Brain.rfa

The brain is a family too; its type is a profile. Staff know the plan. A Visitor knows only what it has walked and seen, and searches door by door: it cannot tell which unseen room is the one it wants.

Sight a door is known once a line from the eyes reaches it; furniture blocks only if taller than the eyes

Search s = curiosity − d/40 − 1.5 (door it came through) + noise 0–0.3; highest first, doors its body cannot pass are dropped

Visitor prior map no · curiosity 0.6 · patience 600 s · pre-movement 30 s

Staff prior map yes · curiosity 0.1 · patience 600 s · pre-movement 15 s

07 / 12 · VA

Engine

Pawn engine

Each body is a disc of its clear width and mass, moved by the social force model every 0.02 s. Results go back to Revit as parameters, a schedule and a map.

m · dv/dt = m (v₀e − v) / τ + Σ fij + Σ fiW

Constants τ = 0.5 s · A = 2000 N · B = 0.08 m

08 / 12

Revit critique

  • No physics. Revit has no bodies, mass, forces or contact. A door is a width in a schedule, not an opening a body has to fit through.
  • No human-readable dynamic experience. Its views are still frames with no time and no movement. It cannot show a walk as a person lives it: where they look, wait, or get lost.
  • No simulation. Nothing runs. Revit cannot answer whether this person can get there, how long it takes, or what they will remember. Here the engine runs outside Revit and writes the answers back.

09 / 12 · Tower

Parametric tower

Project1_mass.rfa · Project1_envelope.rfa

Three numbers shape the mass. Each of the 1,260 panels reads its own width W and sets its louvres, or turns to glass.

zi = z0 + ti (H − z0) · ri = R √(1 − ti²)

Tilt = TiltMin + (TiltMax − TiltMin) · (W − Wmin) / (Wmax − Wmin)

Glazed = W < W_dome
